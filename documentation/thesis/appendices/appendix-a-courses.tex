\chapter{REFERENCE CURRICULUM SHAPE AND SAMPLES}

This appendix records the shape of the Computer Science reference source after import, and gives a
representative sample of its course identities. The full source is retained in the repository with a
recorded origin and a verifier that proves the import is lossless; it is not reproduced here.

The sum of placement credit units is deliberately not treated as a degree total. It counts repeated
elective placements more than once, it includes physical training, and its single uncoded
requirement is a placeholder for credits the source does not name. Every reader therefore treats
the program's credit total as unknown, and the interface withholds a degree percentage rather than
dividing by a number the source does not state.

\section{Repeated Placements}

Fifteen placements repeat a course identity that the source also lists elsewhere. They are stored as
separate placement rows sharing one membership, ordered by their position in the source, so that a
completed course can be claimed by exactly one elective group while remaining visible in the others.
Collapsing them into a single row would lose the group a student's claim refers to; duplicating the
course identity instead would make the rating average and the credit count wrong.
